A specialty, not a sideline

Retiring from a major energy company? We know your benefits.

Corporate retirement packages are complex, and the choices you make in the months around your retirement date can't always be undone. We've walked hundreds of employees through these exact decisions.

Talk to an Advisor
  • 01

    Pension: lump sum or annuity?

    We model both paths against your income needs, interest rates and survivor goals.

  • 02

    401(k) & savings plan rollovers

    Move assets thoughtfully, without triggering avoidable taxes or penalties.

  • 03

    Company stock concentration

    Reduce single-stock risk, including net unrealized appreciation (NUA) strategies where they fit.

  • 04

    Social Security & income timing

    Coordinate when to claim benefits and which accounts to draw from first.

Our approach

A simple process, built on relationships.

  1. 1

    Discover

    We start by listening: your goals, your timeline, your concerns and what matters most to your family.

  2. 2

    Plan

    We build a written plan covering investments, income, taxes and benefits, and walk you through every piece of it.

  3. 3

    Invest

    We implement a portfolio designed for your plan, then manage it with discipline through every market cycle.

  4. 4

    Review

    Life changes. We meet regularly to adjust your plan, and we're a phone call away in between.

Our investment philosophy

What we look for in an investment

Our favorite investments share five characteristics. If a company can't pass all five, we're comfortable waiting.

  1. 01Easy to understand
  2. 02Strong competitive advantages
  3. 03A long runway to earn high returns on reinvested profits
  4. 04Shareholder-friendly management
  5. 05A stock price with a margin of error below what we believe the business is worth
